## ✨ Features & Design Highlights

1. **Uncluttered & Clean UI:**
   - Eliminated redundant repetitive translations on every button and navigation item.
   - Clean, purposeful language division: crisp English for modern layout labels, warm cultured Hindi for blessings and store philosophy.
2. **Sanskrit Shloka & Adornment Philosophy:**
   - Featuring Mahakavi Bhartrihari's celebrated *Niti Shatakam* verse (श्लोक १९):
     `केयूरा न विभूषयन्ति पुरुषं हारा न चन्द्रोज्ज्वलाः...`
   - Complete Hindi meaning (भावार्थ) & brand philosophy explaining that true adornment is trust, integrity, and grace.
3. **Grand Festive Diwali & Dhanteras Showcase:**
   - Authentic festive jewellery photography with brass diyas, Goddess Lakshmi idol, pure 22K gold necklace, and Dhanteras coins.
   - Highlights for Dussehra, Karwa Chauth, Dhanteras, and Diwali.
4. **Our Two Stores Showcase:**
   - Dedicated cards for Patna Chauraha and Gorakhpur stores with direct click-to-call, WhatsApp, and Google Maps links.
5. **Curated Collections & Photo Gallery:**
   - High-resolution jewellery photography with Lightbox modal and direct WhatsApp enquiry.
